Plain words: what is this price? What is a CBAM certificate?通俗解释：这个价格是什么？CBAM 证书是什么？

This is the EU's carbon price.这是欧盟的碳价。 Emitting one tonne of CO₂ in the EU costs money: power plants and factories must hold a permit for every tonne they emit, and permits trade on the EU's carbon market — the Emissions Trading System (ETS). The market price of a permit is the number on this card.在欧盟境内排放一吨 CO₂ 是有成本的：电厂和工厂每排放一吨都必须持有一张配额，配额在欧盟碳市场——碳排放交易体系（ETS）——上交易。配额的市场价格就是本卡片显示的数字。

A CBAM certificate is the import version of an ETS permit.CBAM 证书是 ETS 配额的进口版本。 CBAM — the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism — is the rule that extends the same carbon cost to imported goods: the importer surrenders one CBAM certificate per tonne of embedded CO₂e, at the ETS price, so imported carbon costs the same as EU-made carbon.CBAM（Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism，碳边境调节机制）就是把同样的碳成本延伸到进口商品的规则：进口商按每吨隐含 CO₂e 交出一张 CBAM 证书，价格与 ETS 一致——让进口碳与欧盟本土碳的成本相同。

Who buys, where, when:谁购买、在哪买、何时买： only the authorised EU importer — the company responsible for the declaration — through the EU CBAM Registry, from February 1st, 2027. The price follows the ETS auction average — quarterly in 2026, weekly from 2027. Mills, producers, and trading houses never buy: they supply the data the declaration is built on.只有经授权的欧盟进口商——负责申报的公司——购买：2027 年 2 月 1 日起，通过欧盟 CBAM 登记处购买。价格跟随 ETS 拍卖均价——2026 年按季度，2027 年起按周。工厂、生产商和贸易公司永远不购买：他们只需提供申报所需的数据。

What is CBAM? — the 60-second guide什么是 CBAM？——一分钟读懂

CBAM — the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism — is the EU's charge on carbon in imports.CBAM（碳边境调节机制）是欧盟对进口商品所含碳排放的收费。 From January 1st, 2026, iron & steel, aluminium, cement, fertilisers, hydrogen, and electricity imported into the EU carry a charge on the carbon emitted to produce them.自 2026 年 1 月 1 日起，进口到欧盟的钢铁、铝、水泥、化肥、氢和电力，须为其生产过程排放的碳付费。

The EU importer pays — nobody outside the EU pays the EU directly.由欧盟进口商支付——欧盟以外的工厂和贸易公司不直接向欧盟付款。 The importer buys CBAM certificates — one per tonne of embedded CO₂e, at the EU's carbon-market price — and files one annual declaration.进口商购买 CBAM 证书——每吨隐含 CO₂e 一张，价格按欧盟碳市场价格计算——并每年申报一次。

The bill is computed from the mill's data.账单金额由工厂的数据决定。 Without verified data from the producer, the importer must use Brussels' conservative default values plus a penalty mark-up: +10% in 2026, +20% in 2027, +30% from 2028. Unverified data is the expensive option.若没有生产商提供的经核实数据，进口商只能按欧盟从严设定的默认值加罚金加价计算：2026 年 +10%、2027 年 +20%、2028 年起 +30%。没有数据，就是最贵的选择。

The charge is small now and grows every year.现在的费用很小，但逐年递增。 Certificates go on sale February 1st, 2027; the first annual declaration is due September 30th, 2027; the payable share rises from 2.5% in 2026 to 100% by 2034.证书 2027 年 2 月 1 日开售；首次年度申报 2027 年 9 月 30 日截止；应缴份额从 2026 年的 2.5% 升至 2034 年的 100%。

Kaimeter is the data toolchain for that chain.Kaimeter 就是这条链条的数据工具。 Mills produce verified data packs on their own machine — nothing leaves without approval; traders pass them through; importers file from them. Every step runs on the user's own machine; no Kaimeter server participates at any point.工厂在本机生成经核实的数据包——未经批准绝不外发；贸易公司负责传递；进口商据此申报。每一步都运行在用户自己的机器上，全程没有任何 Kaimeter 服务器参与。
